Who We Are

Shakti Photon Solutions

A deep-tech clean energy company headquartered at IIT Madras Research Park, Chennai. Founded by researchers with doctoral and postdoctoral backgrounds from NUS Singapore, Uppsala University, Oxford, IIT Kanpur, and LANL (Los Alamos National Laboratory), we design and manufacture India's most advanced hydrogen energy systems.

Our mission is to make green hydrogen generation and utilization technology accessible and affordable for Indian industries, research institutions, and the government. Every product we ship is designed, fabricated, and tested in-house — giving our customers full technical accountability and faster support than any import.

We specialize in Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM), Anion Exchange Membrane (AEM), and Alkaline electrolyzers for on-site hydrogen generation; PEM fuel cell systems for clean power; and electrochemical CO₂ reduction systems for converting industrial waste carbon into valuable chemicals. We also offer a unique Equipment as a Service model, giving researchers access to our precision fabrication tools without capital investment.

Our teams operate from Chennai (HQ) at IIT Madras Research Park and from Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, where our R&D lab is located.
